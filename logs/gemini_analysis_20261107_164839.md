### 1. Leak: Preflop Aggression with Marginal Hands (Calling/Raising too loosely)
* **The Evidence:** 
  * In `PREFLOP` at equity bucket `0.4`, `CHECK_CALL` yields an average net of `+0.12 bb` (N=226) and `RAISE_HALF` yields `+6.10 bb` (N=205), whereas `FOLD` loses `-1.86 bb` (N=207). 
  * Similarly, at equity bucket `0.5`, `CHECK_CALL` yields `+12.60 bb` (N=192) and `RAISE_POT` yields `+15.28 bb` (N=152). 
  * The current preflop open threshold is set to `OPEN_PRE = (0.545, 0.60)`. The data shows that hands with equity around 0.4–0.5 are performing remarkably well when played (especially via calling or half-pot raises), meaning our preflop requirements are currently too tight, leaving value on the table.
* **Proposed Change:** Lower the preflop half-pot open threshold to capture more value from these playable marginal equities.
  * **Setting:** `OPEN_PRE`
  * **New Value:** `(0.48, 0.60)` (down from `0.545`)
* **Confirmation Sample Size:** 
  * To reliably detect a shift in winrate of ~3–5 bb/deal against the noise standard deviation of 25 bb, we need a standard two-sample z-test calculation. 
  * $\text{Required } N \approx 16 \times \left(\frac{\sigma}{\Delta}\right)^2 = 16 \times \left(\frac{25}{4}\right)^2 \approx \mathbf{625\text{ hands}}$ in the modified equity bucket range.

### 2. Leak: Over-folding Postflop on the Flop (Equity 0.4)
* **The Evidence:** 
  * On the `FLOP` at equity bucket `0.4`, `FOLD` results in a heavy loss of `-12.43 bb` (N=56), while `CHECK_CALL` loses only `-1.02 bb` (N=102) and `RAISE_HALF` loses `-5.36 bb` (N=91). 
  * The bot is folding too often when it has moderate equity (0.4), incorrectly treating these spots as hopeless when pot odds and implied value actually make calling or semi-bluffing profitable compared to surrendering.
* **Proposed Change:** Lower the `BASE_MARGIN` requirement so that the bot calls and continues more frequently with marginal postflop holdings instead of folding.
  * **Setting:** `BASE_MARGIN`
  * **New Value:** `0.02` (down from `0.03`)
* **Confirmation Sample Size:** 
  * Isolating postflop decisions in the 0.3–0.5 equity bucket range, detecting a 6 bb/deal improvement against a 25 bb noise level requires:
  * $N \approx 16 \times \left(\frac{25}{6}\right)^2 \approx \mathbf{280\text{ postflop decisions}}$ (roughly 600–800 total hands played out to the flop).
