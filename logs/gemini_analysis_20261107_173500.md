### Sample Size Requirements for Confirmation
Because poker hand outcomes have a high standard deviation ($\sigma \approx 25\text{ bb/deal}$), detecting a true mean shift ($\Delta$) requires a robust sample size. The standard formula for sample size in a normal distribution is $N \approx 16 \cdot (\sigma / \Delta)^2$.

1. **Preflop Change (`OPEN_PRE`):** 
   * *Target Delta:* Detecting a shift of ~5 bb/deal.
   * *Calculation:* $16 \times (25 / 5)^2 = 16 \times 25 = 400$ explored action instances. 
   * *Hands Needed:* Since only a fraction of hands reach these specific decision nodes, we would need roughly **4,000 to 5,000 total hands** of duplicate match logs to reliably confirm the preflop adjustment via exploratory data.
2. **Flop Change (`OPEN_POST`):**
   * *Target Delta:* Detecting a shift of ~5 bb/deal (saving ~10 bb on marginal raises).
   * *Calculation:* Same variance profile ($\sigma \approx 25$, $\Delta = 5$).
   * *Hands Needed:* Approximately **4,000 total hands** to accumulate enough instances in the 0.3–0.5 equity buckets.
3. **Turn/Postflop Reraise Change (`RERAISE_POST`):**
   * *Target Delta:* Reraising errors on the turn are expensive ($\Delta \approx 8\text{ bb/deal}$). 
   * *Calculation:* $16 \times (25 / 8)^2 \approx 156$ instances. 
   * *Hands Needed:* Turn decisions occur less frequently than preflop/flop. To get ~150 explored instances specifically facing turn bets, we would need approximately **6,000 to 8,000 total hands**, or alternatively rely on a shorter validation run if we use direct A/B testing across the 2,000-hand match structure.
