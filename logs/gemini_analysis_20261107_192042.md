Based on the Snowflake logs and the current bot settings, here are the 3 most critical leaks, concrete parameter adjustments, and sample size requirements.

---

### 1. Leak: Over-raising on the Flop with medium-to-weak equities (0.3–0.4)
* **The Numbers:** In the FLOP 0.3 equity bucket, `RAISE_HALF` averages **-15.78 bb** and `RAISE_POT` averages **-16.51 bb**, compared to **-8.81 bb** for `CHECK_CALL` and **-10.68 bb** for `FOLD`. Similarly, in the 0.4 equity bucket, `RAISE_POT` loses **-9.60 bb** while `CHECK_CALL` loses only **-1.02 bb**. 
* **The Issue:** Our postflop open/reraise thresholds are set too aggressively for marginal holdings, causing us to inflate pots when behind on the board.
* **Proposed Change:** Increase `OPEN_POST` for half-pot raises to require more strength.
  * **Setting:** `OPEN_POST = (0.65, 0.75)` *(up from `0.60, 0.75`)*

### 2. Leak: Under-raising Preflop in the 0.4 Equity Bucket
* **The Numbers:** In the PREFLOP 0.4 equity bucket, `RAISE_HALF` yields an average net of **+6.10 bb** (N=205), significantly outperforming `CHECK_CALL` (**+0.12 bb**, N=226), `FOLD` (**-1.86 bb**), and `RAISE_POT` (**-4.95 bb**). 
* **The Issue:** Preflop equities around 0.4 perform surprisingly well with half-pot aggression (likely due to fold equity and position in Short Deck PLO), but our current `OPEN_PRE` threshold is set higher at **0.545**, meaning we aren't opening enough hands with a half-pot size when we hold 0.4 equity.
* **Proposed Change:** Lower the preflop half-pot open threshold to capture this profitable aggression.
  * **Setting:** `OPEN_PRE = (0.38, 0.60)` *(down from `0.545, 0.60`)*

### 3. Leak: Over-bluffing/Raising on the Turn with weak equities (0.2–0.3)
* **The Numbers:** On the TURN with 0.3 equity, `RAISE_HALF` loses **-13.87 bb** (N=47) and `RAISE_POT` loses **-18.89 bb** (N=61), whereas `CHECK_CALL` only loses **-1.67 bb** (N=49). Even at 0.2 equity, `RAISE_HALF` and `RAISE_POT` bleed chips (-6.49 to -19.08 bb).
* **The Issue:** The bot is firing too many aggressive bets/raises on later streets when equity has degraded, likely compounded by a `BLUFF_RATE` of 0.20 that triggers too often when opponents aren't folding enough.
* **Proposed Change:** Reduce the `BLUFF_RATE` to cut down unprofitable turn/river aggression with weak holdings.
  * **Setting:** `BLUFF_RATE = 0.10` *(down from `0.20`)*

### Sample Size Requirements for Confirmation
Given that hand outcomes have a standard deviation ($\sigma$) of roughly **25 bb per deal**, the standard error of the mean difference ($\Delta$) between two actions is $\text{SE} = \frac{\sigma \sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{N}} = \frac{35.36}{\sqrt{N}}$. To detect a meaningful shift of $\ge 5\text{ bb}$ with 95% confidence ($Z = 1.96$), we need:
$$N \approx \left( \frac{1.96 \times 35.36}{5} \right)^2 \approx 193 \text{ explored samples per action category}.$$

* **For Leak 1 (`OPEN_POST` adjustment):** We want to verify the shift away from raising at 0.3–0.4 flop equity. We have roughly ~100 samples per action currently, so we need about **200 explored samples** in those specific buckets to confirm the net improvement.
* **For Leak 2 (`OPEN_PRE` adjustment):** With N=205 already supporting the 0.4 preflop half-raise, we have enough baseline data, but we need roughly **200 new explored samples** under the *new* setting to verify stability.
* **For Leak 3 (`BLUFF_RATE` adjustment):** Turn data is sparser (N=33 to 61 per action). To reliably confirm a reduction in variance-heavy turn bluffing leaks, we require at least **250–300 explored turn decisions**. 

*Note: Because matches are 2,000 hands total, full confirmation of all three tweaks simultaneously will require running approximately **3 to 5 full match iterations** of 2,000 hands with EXPLORED=1 enabled.*
